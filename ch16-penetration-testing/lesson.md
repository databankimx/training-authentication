# 16. Penetration Testing of Authentication

A penetration test asks whether a system can be broken into by someone who is trying. For authentication, that means looking at how sign-in, tokens, and sessions can be abused. This chapter covers what makes a test lawful, the flaws that testers most often find, and the defences a developer builds in before a tester ever arrives.

The chapter contains no exploitation steps and no attack code. It describes the flaws at the level a developer needs in order to defend against them.

The chapter has two reading steps, a sample step, a code homework item, a written homework item, and a summary with three quizzes.
