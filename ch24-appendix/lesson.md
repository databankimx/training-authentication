# Appendix

This appendix is reference material. Use it after the chapters, or on the job, to find a rule quickly. Each part is short, and each one points back to the chapter that explains it.

It has four parts: a cheat sheet of the checks and status codes, a glossary of the terms used in the tutorial, a pointer to the identity platform comparison in Chapter 12, and a guide to what to do when a request is refused or a credential is leaked.
