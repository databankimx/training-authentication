# 7. Sign-In Strategies

So far the tutorial has mostly been about a script with its own credential. Most people, though, sign in to applications rather than holding a token themselves. This chapter covers how an application can hand sign-in to a central identity provider, how the common protocols carry that sign-in, how a session starts and ends, and how accounts are created and removed. Those last two matter most when someone leaves.

The chapter has five reading steps, one sample, two homework items, and a summary with three quizzes.
