# 0. Introduction

Almost every script that talks to another system has to answer one question first: who is asking? The answer is a credential, and how you handle that credential decides whether your automation is safe to run, safe to share, and safe to leave running while you're away.

This chapter sets up the ideas and the way the tutorial works. It has two reading steps, and a summary with three quizzes. There's no homework in this chapter, so you won't be asked to write code yet.

The tutorial covers both sides of the problem. On the client side, your script proves who it is to someone else's API. On the server side, your own API decides who may call it. The same ideas sit under both, and you'll meet them in all three languages the collection uses.
