# 15. Audits and Compliance

Sooner or later, someone outside the team will ask how a script signs in, who can run it, and what happens when a person leaves. That someone is an auditor, and the questions come from a framework the organization has agreed to meet. This chapter explains what an audit checks, the frameworks you are most likely to meet, and the evidence that answers the questions about authentication.

This chapter describes common practice. It isn't legal or compliance advice. Your team's compliance function decides which frameworks apply, and which of their requirements are in scope.

The chapter has three reading steps, two written homework items, and a summary with three quizzes.
