# 8. The Client Credentials Flow

Most of the flows in the last chapter have a person at the keyboard. A scheduled job, a background service, or an integration between two systems has no one to sign in. It needs its own identity, and the client credentials flow gives it one. The script presents a client identifier and a secret to the token endpoint, asks for the scopes it needs, and receives an access token it can use.

The chapter has a reading step, a sample step, a code homework item, a written homework item, and a summary with three quizzes.
