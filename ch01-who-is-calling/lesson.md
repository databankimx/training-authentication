# 1. Who Is Calling

Every request your script sends to an API says who is calling, one way or another. Most of the time that statement is a header with a credential in it. This chapter explains what that header is, what it can and can't prove, and how to build it yourself so you know exactly what goes over the wire.

The chapter has two reading steps, a sample step, two homework items, and a summary with three quizzes. The homework is where you build a header yourself, so take your time with it.
