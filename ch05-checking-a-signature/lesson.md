# 5. Checking a Signature

The last chapter showed that a token can be read by anyone. This chapter shows how a verifier knows the token hasn't been changed since it was issued, and what that knowledge costs. The check is a calculation, and it's simple enough to do by hand once, which is the best way to understand why it works.

The chapter has two reading steps, a sample step, a second reading step, two homework items, and a summary with three quizzes.
