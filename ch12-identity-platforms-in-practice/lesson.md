# 12. Identity Platforms in Practice

The concepts in the last eleven chapters appear, under different names, in every identity platform you're likely to use. A service identity in one product is a service principal in another, and a scope in one is a permission on a role in a third. This chapter lines the common platforms up against each other, so you can read any of their documentation with the concepts already in hand.

The chapter has a reading step, a comparison step, a written homework item, a second written homework item, and a summary with three quizzes.
