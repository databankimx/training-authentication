# Summary: Identity Platforms in Practice

This chapter showed that the concepts of this tutorial appear in every major identity platform, under different names. You've seen how to find the question behind a vendor's term, how the comparison table lines the platforms up, and what to confirm before you rely on any of them.

## What you learned

- Every identity platform answers the same questions: what the application's identity is, what a workload's identity is, who issues the tokens, what they may ask for, and how long access lasts.
- The names differ because each platform grew from a different starting point, and the concepts are the same.
- The same term can mean different things in different products, so check what it does in yours.
- The comparison table is a map of where to look, and the vendor documentation is the authority.
- A claim about a platform is a claim about a version, an edition, and a configuration, so confirm it in the environment you will use.

## Quizzes

Three questions, one for each big idea in the chapter. Pick an answer, and read the explanation whichever way it goes.

```quiz
You read a vendor's documentation and meet a term you don't recognize. What is the best first step?
○ Assume it means the same as the term you know best, and carry on.
→ Terms are reused with different meanings. Check what the term does in that product.
◉ Find which concept it answers, such as the token issuer or the permission a token carries.
→ Right. Asking which question the term answers is the quickest route to its meaning.
○ Skip the term, because the important parts of a platform are its screens.
→ The screens change often. The concepts behind the terms are what carry over between platforms.
```

```quiz
A platform's documentation says that a feature is available. What should you confirm before relying on it?
◉ That the feature is available in your version, edition, and configuration.
→ Right. A claim about a platform is a claim about a version, an edition, and a configuration.
○ That the feature exists in every other platform as well.
→ Features differ between platforms. Confirm the one you are using.
○ That the feature has been available for many years.
→ Age does not show that a feature is present in your environment today.
```

```quiz

◉ Because platforms rename features and change defaults, so the vendor's own page is the reliable source.
```
